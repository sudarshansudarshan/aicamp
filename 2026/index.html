What You'll Learn

Six weeks. Six transformations.

Each week builds on the last — from mathematical intuition to building real AI systems using the Vicharanashala stack.

Week 01
Reset & Foundation
"Unlearn what you think you know."
  • Mathematical thinking — slow and deep
  • Probability without formulas
  • German Tank, Birthday Paradox, Monty Hall
  • Linear Algebra through GeoGebra
  • Daily ritual: 1 unsolvable problem, 2 hours
Week 02
Classical AI Reimagined
"Before the hype, there was genius."
  • PageRank — how a dorm idea became Google
  • Perceptron — the first neuron
  • Huffman Encoding — compression as art
  • Closest Pairs, Min-Cut, Vigenere Cipher
  • Project seed: Tell the story of one algorithm
Week 03
Machine Learning by Instinct
"Feel the data before you fit it."
  • Bayesian thinking through market games
  • Decision Trees and why they break
  • SVMs — the best margin you'll ever see
  • Dimensionality reduction, Recommender Systems
  • Build a classifier on real data you bring
Week 04
The Deep Learning Layer
"Networks that see, hear, and feel."
  • Neural networks — forward pass as intuition
  • CNNs through image puzzles
  • RNNs and the memory problem
  • LSTMs in the wild
  • Guest: practitioner on what breaks in production
Week 05
The LLM Revolution
"You're living through the biggest shift in computing."
  • Transformers — attention as democracy of context
  • Prompt engineering as a craft
  • Building with APIs — Ajrasakha live demo
  • AI Agents and tool use
  • Workshop: Build an agent on the AIVS stack
Week 06
Build, Ship, Present
"Don't just learn. Make something."
  • Teams of 2, one real-world project each
  • Domains: agriculture, education, health, governance
  • Mentoring by DLED faculty + AWaDH founders
  • Public Demo Day — open to campus
  • Top performers: fast-track Vinternship invite

FAQ

Questions answered.

Do I need prior AI or programming knowledge?
None whatsoever. All you need is the intention to learn and the willingness to think slowly. We've had students from arts, commerce, and humanities backgrounds who thrived here.
How is this different from an online course?
You live here. You discuss at midnight. You argue in the lab. You fail in front of people who are also failing. The IIT Ropar residential experience is the course — the curriculum is just the structure around it.
What is the selection process?
Register via the form. You'll be shortlisted based on your SOP — we look for curiosity and honesty, not grades. Shortlisted students do a 15-minute online conversation (not a test). Final selections by May 10th. Max 2 students per college, no cap for IIT Madras BS students.
Can I attend online instead of in-person?
Yes — we have 50 online seats at ₹5,000. You get live-streamed lectures, full ViBe access, and virtual roundtable participation. Top 5 online performers are invited to Demo Day on campus with travel support.
What is the NHEQF credit and how does it work?
AIVS 2026 is designed as a 1-credit course under the National Higher Education Qualification Framework. Upon completion, you receive a credit letter from IIT Ropar that can be recognised by NHEQF-aligned institutions. Details will be shared with selected students.
How do I reach IIT Ropar?
By air: Chandigarh International Airport (56 km). By rail: Ropar Railway Station (7.5 km) or Chandigarh (59 km). From Chandigarh, hire a cab or take a bus to Ropar Bus Stand, then an auto to campus. For late arrivals, contact us — we'll help.
What happens after the camp?
Top 20% of students receive a fast-track invitation to the next Vicharanashala Vinternship cohort. You also join the growing AIVS alumni network. AWaDH will facilitate startup connections for those interested.
